MATH70 - Linear Algebra

Category: Mathematics | Date: Oct 2026

Linear algebra is the study of vectors, matrices, and linear transformations. It is foundational for many areas of mathematics, engineering, physics, and computer science.

Key Topics

  • Vectors and vector spaces
  • Systems of linear equations
  • Matrix operations and inverses
  • Determinants and eigenvalues
  • Orthogonality and projections

Important Idea

Many real-world problems can be modeled as linear systems, where matrix methods help solve, interpret, and transform the data.
